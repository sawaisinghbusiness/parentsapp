# Parents Home refinement

Scope: the existing Parents Home, retaining the school photograph, real logo, navy actions, child switcher, Hindi/English support and current routes.

Reading order: school identity, selected child and today's attendance, quick navigation, current updates, notices and events. School data is the authority; missing records never become sample records. Cached attendance is shown as today only when its date matches Asia/Kolkata.

Use a 20px gutter, 24px between major groups, 12px within rows, and 12–14px card corners. Charcoal text and a warm stone-to-paper gradient support navy actions. The school photograph fades into stone (#F3EEE6), transitioning through #F7F4EF to paper (#FAF8F4). The child card stays white; navigation shortcuts and update sections sit on the page surface. Fees payable uses the same neutral row styling as homework and results, with its amount and payment link retained. Green/red attendance text always includes the written status.

Use the existing Plus Jakarta Sans with Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi. Keep meaningful controls at least 44px high. Allow long names and notices to wrap. Respect reduced-motion settings.

Impeccable 4.9.0 was used for the layout/craft review. Its common-font detector warning is intentionally accepted to preserve the app's established typeface. The warm stone Home background and removal of amber fee emphasis were approved by the user on 2026-11-14. Invented status thresholds and decorative badges remain outside scope.
