# Parents app interface

The approved Home, Fees, Calendar and Study screens are the visual authority. Extend their existing Plus Jakarta Sans / Noto Sans Devanagari typography, navy navigation, light canvas and white work surfaces. Ordinary save/pay/submit actions use the shared evergreen #294E43 palette. Keep the distinct Calendar filled date states and Study burnt-orange/neutral treatment.

This is an operating app for parents: prioritize the correct child, understandable school information and a clear next action. Outlined status labels use dark text and trailing coloured dots. Do not spread status colours across large panels.

Preserve the restored AI Assistant visuals, Search glow and typed hint, real school imagery, Study minute-ruler gestures and four-round progress. Official documents retain their own typography. The existing viewport policy is user-pinned.

Controls need persistent names, useful touch areas, keyboard focus and recovery. Photo dialogs trap and restore focus, support Escape and maintain nested scroll locks. Bottom-navigation labels wrap when text grows. Forms guard unsaved reference/reason entry. A failed search source is unavailable information, not no results; saved content has a visible refresh-failure notice. Keep bilingual copy and permission behavior.

Evidence: desktop repo docs/apps-charles-audit-2026-11-15/IMPLEMENTATION.md. Browser fixtures isolate review from real submissions; native-device accessibility and live backend behavior need separate verification.
